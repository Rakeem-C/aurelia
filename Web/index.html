Economy & advancement
Workers gather spatial resources and carry them to drop-off buildings. Select workers to place structures; right click damaged friendly buildings to repair. Select production buildings to train, research or advance. Cancel a queue item with its × button.
Touch & victory
Tap to select. Use Context order then tap a target or ground. Use two fingers to pan. Minimap navigation and toolbar commands remain available. Destroy the opposing civic heart and remaining military to win.
Autosave runs every minute and when your age changes. Cheats must be enabled before a match.
